# UX sweep — 16 September 2026

A designer's pass over Thready or Not as it stands at cache v16: every screen and sheet
in all three crafts, all six themes, at 375×812 (spot-checked at 375×640 and 760×900).

**Method.** The app was driven in the Browser pane with real data — the Sheep template plus a
pasted amigurumi pattern, `tmp-pdf/xs-pacman.pdf` through the colour-key parser *and* the beta
grid reader, `tmp-pdf/xs-pokemon-tr.pdf` for a 98-colour key, and `tmp-pdf/sew-sundress.pdf`
with pages kept. Contrast was computed in the page with a relative-luminance helper against the
composited background of each text node, not eyeballed; touch targets and overflow came from a
DOM scan of every interactive element's bounding box. Every number below is measured.

**Headline.** Contrast was the big one: 4 of 6 themes failed WCAG AA on their primary button,
3 of 6 on secondary text, and the selected state of every tab, pill and segmented control sat at
2.0–2.2:1 in the light themes. The crochet counter hid its own `−1` and `reset stitches` buttons
below the fold at the most common phone height. Both craft tours were silent no-ops when started
from Settings, and marked themselves as seen anyway.

After this pass: **zero** contrast failures on home, the crochet counter, the cross-stitch screen,
the sewing screen and all 8 cross-stitch sheets, in all six themes, and **zero** touch targets
under 44 px apart from two deliberate 40×44 controls (F31).

**Counts:** 11 high · 42 medium · 21 low = 74 findings. 63 fixed in this pass, 11 written up below.

## Needs a logic change or a decision

**D1 — Cross-stitch chart canvas has no keyboard path (high).**
`js/app-xstitch.js:418` sets `role="img"` / `aria-label="Chart"` on a canvas that handles
`pointerdown`, `pointerup`, wheel zoom, pinch, drag-paint, 10×10 and the eraser. Marking a
specific square is pointer-only; the zoom buttons are the sheet's only keyboard-reachable
controls. Recommendation: give the canvas `tabindex="0"` and `role="application"`, keep a cursor
cell in view state, move it with the arrow keys, mark with Enter/Space, and rewrite `aria-label`
each move to "Row 12, column 40, DMC 310, not stitched". That is a real feature, not a sweep edit.

**D2 — Photo-crop handles announce as buttons and cannot be used (high).**
`js/app-xstitch.js:3827` creates four `<span role="button">` corner handles with `aria-label` but
no `tabindex` and no keydown, and `js/app-xstitch.js:3977` is a `role="group"` crop box that only
responds to drags. Either add `tabindex="0"` plus arrow-key nudging (8 px, 1 px with Shift), or
drop the `role`/`aria-label` so they stop advertising themselves and lean on the existing
"Reset crop" button. The minimal honest fix is the second; the good one is the first.

**D3 — The `showModal` fallback is a broken sheet (high).**
`js/app.js:341` falls back to `dlg.setAttribute('open','')`, which yields a non-modal dialog: no
top layer, no focus trap, no `::backdrop`, and `if (e.target === dlg)` never fires so the
backdrop tap does nothing. The app targets iOS PWAs where this path is reachable. This pass made
Escape work on that path; a trap and a backdrop element need a real decision — the simplest is to
render an explicit `.sheet-backdrop` div and a focus trap whenever `showModal` is absent.

**D4 — The counter still scrolls below ~700 px of viewport (low).**
Fixed at 812; at 640 the content is 93 px over even with the button at its floor. Options: let the
counter card shrink (it is 146 px and mostly whitespace), or move `.stitch-actions` above the
button. Both change the visual order, so this is the owner's call.

**D5 — Focus is lost on every `rerender()` (medium).**
Nothing in `app.js`, `app-xstitch.js` or `app-sewing.js` captures `document.activeElement` before
rebuilding a list. Ticking three steps in the Steps sheet drops focus to `<body>` three times; the
same happens on cutting chips and page thumbnails. Recommendation: a shared
`ctx.preserveFocus(fn)` helper that records the focused element's `data-focus-key` and restores it
after the rebuild — worth doing once in `app.js` so all three crafts get it.

**D6 — Tour overlay accessibility (medium).** `js/tour.js` is copy-only for this pass. Three
things: the card carries `role="dialog"` **and** `aria-live="polite"` so its content is announced
twice; `destroyUI()` removes the node holding focus and never restores it; and when no `<dialog>`
is open the page behind the overlay stays in the tab order (there is a `trapTab` on the card, but
nothing pulls focus back if it escapes). Also `reducedMotion()` at `js/tour.js:42` is defined and
never called. Separately, `ensureProject()` at `js/tour.js:604` does `Store.projects()[0]` with no
craft filter, so the **counter** tour lands on `#screen-craft` and drops all twelve steps if a
cross-stitch or sewing project happens to be first — the same class of bug as H6, and it needs a
one-line craft filter in `tour.js`.

**D7 — Decide one announcement policy (medium).** Today: crochet announces every row and never a
stitch; cross-stitch announces every 100th stitch; sewing announces every block bump and every
step. Suggestion: announce at group boundaries and milestones in every craft (row done, group
done, colour done, step done, every 50th stitch), and never per stitch — per-stitch would make the
big button unusable with a screen reader on.

**D8 — "SA 1 cm (3/8")" on the sewing step screen (low).** The abbreviation is unexplained and
the expansion does not fit 375 px beside the page chip. Options: drop the page chip to an icon and
write "Seam allowance 1 cm", or keep "SA" and add a one-line explainer the first time a sewing
project is opened.

**D9 — Home card summaries read as three different products (low).** "Body · Rnd 0 · 5/6 sts" /
"0 of 10 colours · 22 chart pages · 0%" / "GATHERED SUNDRESS # 1301 · step 1 of 15 · 0 of 5 cut".
Suggestion: a shared shape of *where you are · how far through*, sentence-cased, with the pattern
name title-cased rather than passed through from the PDF, and "not started yet" instead of
"Rnd 0". The crochet and cross-stitch summaries live in `js/app.js` and `js/app-xstitch.js`; the
sewing one pulls its name from `js/sewing.js`.

**D10 — A 98-colour key is 9,157 px of scrolling (medium).** Real charts hit this: the Pokémon
fixture imports 98 colours and the key card alone is thirteen screens. The current colour is only
identifiable from the bar at the top, which scrolls away. Suggestions, cheapest first: make the
current-colour bar sticky; add a "not finished yet" filter to the existing sort pills; collapse
finished colours behind a count. A virtualised list would be the last resort.

**D11 — Release hygiene.** This pass changed four CSS files and four JS files. `sw.js` is
cache-first and outside this pass's ownership: **bump `CACHE_VERSION` from v16 to v17** before
deploying, or returning users will keep the old stylesheet. No new files were added, so the
precache list does not need changing.
