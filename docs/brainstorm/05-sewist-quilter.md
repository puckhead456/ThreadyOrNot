# Brainstorm 05 — the sewist and quilter

Thirty minutes in the sewing module at 375×812 with two Pattern Runway booklets (`sew-sundress.pdf`, `sew-pencilskirt.pdf`) imported through the craft's import sheet, plus a synthetic Riley-Blake-style quilt pattern run through `Sewing.parse` and loaded into a project, because there is still no quilt fixture.

The skeleton is right and the import genuinely fills itself — 15 steps, 5 notions, a size chart, two fabric widths and a seam allowance with its exception, all from one drop, all on-device. What it does not yet do is survive contact with the machine: a "step" here is a whole booklet paragraph (627 chars, six separate operations) so "step 1 of 15" tells me nothing about where my hands are; four of those fifteen steps are the marketing page ("Layer with lace"), so the app will congratulate me four steps after the dress is finished; and both booklets put their cutting list on the pattern sheets, so I am left with the Garment template's invented Front/Back/Sleeve/Facing while sewing a bodice-waistband-skirt-pocket dress.

For quilting it is worse than "untested": the fabric-requirements block parses to `[]` because every fabric line in a quilt pattern is a dot leader with no bolt width, and the cutting list is a 56-tap-per-row chip grouped under a heading called "Other".

Almost all of the fixes are small, and the two best ones are already built — the importer's ✂ sentence splitter and the paragraph picker just aren't reachable once you've pressed Import.

---

## Proposals, ranked by impact ÷ effort

### 1. Let me split a step after import — the ✂ already exists
**Problem.** Sundress step 1 on the step card is 627 characters and contains six machine operations: join side front to centre front, repeat other side, join side back to centre back, repeat, press all seams open over a ham, repeat the lot for the lining, then stitch the right side seam of the shell and the left of the lining. I put the phone down after "press all seams open" and when I pick it up there is one button, STEP DONE, and no memory of which half I did. Pencil skirt step 10 is 796 characters. Meanwhile the import sheet's **Make steps from paragraphs → ✂** already offers a perfect sentence-level split list ("Draw a diagonal line…", "Sew on the drawn line.", "Trim 1/4" from the seam…", "Make 80.") — and it is unreachable the moment you press Import.
**Proposal.** Add a `✂ Split this step` action to each row of the Steps sheet and to the step card's ⋯/long-press, reusing `openParagraphPicker`'s existing "start a new step at…" sentence list. Splitting step *i* replaces it with *k* steps in place, keeps `page` and `section`, marks steps before the chosen split point done if the original was done, and pushes one undo snapshot. Also auto-offer it: when a step is over ~350 chars, show a small `✂ too long?` chip under the step card the first two times it is seen.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 5 · The picker code is already written and tested; this is a call-site plus a merge into `craftData.steps`.
**Risks.** Re-import merge matches `done` flags on normalised step text — split steps will no longer match and will lose their ticks. Match on a `sourceKey` (original step's normalised text + split index) instead.

### 2. Stop counting the "Pattern variations" pages as steps
**Problem.** The Sundress imports as **15 steps**. Steps 12–15 are "Altering the Skirt Length", "Gathered Skirt", "Layer with lace", "Contrast fabric and colours" — the make-it-your-own marketing page. Step 15's text even ends `E S T .` (the letter-spaced logo). The parser *already knows*: the Steps sheet shows them under a section header `PATTERN VARIATIONS`. But the progress strip says "Step 11 of 15 · 73%" when the dress is finished, and the project-done celebration fires four taps late — or never, because nobody taps through four suggestions they aren't doing.
**Proposal.** In `Sewing.parse`, tag a step section as `optional: true` when its heading matches `/variation|alternative|make it your own|customi[sz]|inspiration|care|washing|share|hashtag|about (the|us)|copyright/i`. Exclude optional steps from `steps.length` in the progress strip, the home-card summary and the completion trigger; render them in the Steps sheet under a collapsed "Variations (4) — not counted" group with a "count these too" toggle.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** A booklet that genuinely titles a construction section "Alternative closure" would get demoted — hence the toggle, and never delete them.

### 3. Cutting chips need a number, not 56 taps
**Problem.** The quilt cutting sheet row `0/56 · 4 1/2" square · Fabric A` is a tap-to-cycle chip. I tapped it three times and got `3/56`. Fifty-three to go, standing at the cutting mat with a rotary cutter in one hand. The HST row is `0/160`. Tap-to-cycle is right for `0/2` on a sleeve and completely wrong above about 6. There is a per-group "Cut all" but no per-row one, and no way to say "I cut 40 of the 56 and ran out of fabric".
**Proposal.** In the cutting sheet, when `qty > 6` replace the cycle chip with a chip that opens a tiny number pad / stepper row (`−10 −1 [40] +1 +10 · all · none`), and always add a per-row long-press → "all cut / none cut". Keep tap-to-cycle for `qty ≤ 6`. Also fix the header: it says "0 of 6 pieces cut" while 3 of 56 squares are cut — count cut *units* (`Σ cutCount` of `Σ qty`), which is what a sewist means by "pieces".
**Effort** S · **Impact** 5 · **Risks.** Two interaction models on one list; distinguish them visually (cycle chip vs chip with a `▸`).

### 4. Quilt fabric requirements parse to nothing
**Problem.** My synthetic Riley Blake block —
`Fabric A (Background) .......... 2 1/4 yards` / `Binding ... 5/8 yard` / `Backing ... 3 3/4 yards` / `Batting ... 68" x 80"` — produced `parse.fabric === []`, with no warning about it. Quilt yardage never names a bolt width (WOF ≈ 42" is assumed), and the fabric parser only starts a `FabricRow` on a line containing a width (`115 cm`, `45"`). So the quilter's entire shopping information vanishes silently. The same rule is why the known bug "`LINING 1m / 1 yd` rows are dropped" exists on the garment side — the Sundress lists "Matching linings" as a notion but has no lining yardage row.
**Proposal.** Add a second `FabricRow` shape inside a FABRIC/REQUIREMENTS block: `^(.{2,40}?)\s*(?:—|-|:)?\s*((?:\d[\d ./]*)\s*(?:yards?|yds?|m|metres?|fat quarters?|FQ|precut|jelly roll)|\d+"\s*[x×]\s*\d+")\s*$` → `{ name, width: '', amounts: [amount] }`, running after the width rule so garment tables are untouched. Emit `warnings.push('No fabric requirements found')` when the block exists but yields nothing. Treat `Backing`, `Batting`, `Binding` as fabric rows, not notions.
**Effort** S–M · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** Dot leaders are already collapsed to ` — ` by `normalizeText`, so match on that separator too. Needs the real Riley Blake / Suzy Quilts PDFs to tune (see infrastructure).

### 5. Put the fabric I still have to buy on the shopping list
**Problem.** `📋 Copy shopping list` produced exactly this:
```
GATHERED SUNDRESS # 1301 — still to buy
• 1x 40cm (15 3/4") Invisible zip
• 1 x Hook and eye
• Matching linings
• Lightweight Fusing
• Piping if desired or piping cord and extra fabric to make own piping
```
The app knows I am size 42 on 115 cm goods and that the pattern wants **2.4 m** — the Fabric sheet lays it out per size beautifully — and it is not on the list. Fabric is the reason I am in the shop; the zip is the afterthought. Thread isn't there either, and no pattern lists it.
**Proposal.** Prepend a Fabric block to the copied text from `fabric[]` filtered to `size.chosen` (`• 2.4 m of 115 cm / 45" main fabric`), fall back to the raw line when amounts didn't align, and append a standing `• Thread to match` when nothing in notions matches `/thread/i`. Add a "what to include" row of toggles (Fabric / Notions / Thread) above the button.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** None material; keep the strings verbatim, never convert units.

### 6. Heading-only "steps" are not steps
**Problem.** Sundress steps 4 (`Waistband:`) and 7 (`Skirt:`) are section labels that got numbered. The step card renders a 93 px card containing the single word "Waistband:" above a 211 px STEP DONE button. Steps 5 and 6 are `SHELL:` and `LINING:` — sub-labels of the waistband, which is why the Steps sheet shows a garbled section header `BODICE SHELL BODICE LINING` (two columns merged).
**Proposal.** In `parseSteps`, when a step's text after the marker is ≤ 40 chars, ends in `:` and has no verb from the sewing action vocabulary (`sew|press|pin|stitch|fold|turn|topstitch|clip|trim|attach|insert|gather|baste|…`), promote it to the current `section` instead of emitting a step, and hang the following steps off it. Two booklets out of two hit this, so it is not an edge case.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** `Hem:` alone as a genuine one-line step would be swallowed; require the *next* line to be a step marker before promoting.

### 7. When the import finds no cutting list, say so on the cutting list
**Problem.** Both Pattern Runway booklets put the cutting list on the pattern sheets, so the parser correctly found none and warned "No cutting list found — add the pieces yourself." Then the project screen said **"0 of 5 pieces cut"** and the cutting sheet listed Front / Back / Sleeve ×2 / Facing–Neckband / Interfacing — the Garment *template's* invented pieces, presented with total authority, for a dress that has a bodice, a bodice lining, a waistband shell and lining, a skirt, pocket facings and pocket bags. There is no sleeve in this pattern.
**Proposal.** Mark template-seeded cutting rows `fromTemplate: true`. When an import finds no cutting list and template rows are still untouched (`cutCount === 0`), replace the sheet body with a one-tap card: *"This pattern keeps its cutting list on the pattern sheets. Your list still has the 5 starter pieces from the Garment template — [Clear them] [Keep them]"*, plus a scan of `sourceText` for capitalised piece-shaped nouns near "cut"/"fold" offered as suggestions. Same treatment on the home-card summary (don't say "0 of 5 cut" for pieces the pattern never mentioned).
**Effort** S · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** Don't auto-clear; a bag sewist may have deliberately started from the template.

### 8. Group quilt cutting by the fabric it comes from
**Problem.** The quilt cutting sheet groups as `CONTRAST · 1 PIECE` (the binding strips, caught by the word "binding") and `OTHER · 5 PIECES` (everything else). Each row carries the right `note` — `Fabric A`, `Fabric B` — but it's a grey sub-label, not the grouping. A quilter cuts by fabric: you pull Fabric A off the shelf and cut everything from it before touching Fabric B. The current grouping makes you hunt.
**Proposal.** When `kind === 'quilt'`, group the cutting sheet by `note` (the `From Fabric X,` source) with `material` demoted to a pill, and add a per-group total (`Fabric A · 3 cuts · 2 1/4 yd`). Keep material grouping for garment/bag. Also stop routing binding into "Contrast" when a fabric source was found on the line.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** Patterns that write `From the background fabric, cut…` need the source label normalised (title-case, strip "the").

### 9. Let me drop the PDF while creating a sewing project
**Problem.** The New project sheet shows the craft picker, then for Sewing the whole `.new-pdf` block — drop zone, paste box, "Also save as a template" — is hidden (`display:none`, confirmed in the DOM). Crochet's headline v19/v20 feature is "drop a PDF straight into the New project sheet"; sewing makes you create an empty project, find the ⋯ menu, tap Import pattern, then drop. On top of that the craft picker sits *below* Name and Emoji, the emoji row is the crochet animal set (🐑🐄🐖🐔🐰🐉) with 🪡/👜/🛏️ at the far end, and the Notes placeholder on a sewing project reads "Hook 4mm · Paintbox DK · pattern link…".
**Proposal.** Move the Craft segmented control to the top of the sheet. When Sewing is selected, mount the shared drop zone wired to `app-sewing.js`'s importer (name, designer and size pre-fill from `parse.meta`, exactly as the review list already does post-hoc). Let a craft supply `emojiSet` and a notes placeholder — the research already specified `emojiSet: ['🪡','🧵','👗','👚','👖','🧥','👜','🎒','🛏️','✂️','📐','🧷']`.
**Effort** M · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** The New project sheet is shell-owned (`js/app.js`); needs the craft contract to grow two optional fields. Keep the crochet path byte-identical.

### 10. Render the page images the steps actually point at
**Problem.** I ticked "Keep the pages so you can see the diagrams" on a 9-page booklet. Rendering runs 1→9 behind a modal you cannot leave, and I stopped it after 3 pages using the (good) "Stop — keep the pages so far". What I kept: cover, size chart, fabric table. **Every single step is on pages 6–9.** The step card's `📄 page 6` chip is rendered dead (`sw-page-plain`) because that page isn't stored, and the only way to get pages 6–9 is to re-run the whole import over the top of my progress.
**Proposal.** Order the render queue by `uniq(steps.map(s => s.page))` first, then the rest ascending, so a stop-early keeps the diagrams. Make the dead page chip tappable anyway → "Page 6 isn't saved — [render it now]" and render that single page on demand from a re-picked file. Show the queue as "Rendering the step pages (3 of 4)…" so the ordering is visible.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 3 · **Risks.** `PdfText.open` already exposes `renderPage(n)`, so on-demand single-page render is cheap; the re-pick of the file is the friction (browsers won't keep the handle).

### 11. Show the pattern's own step number
**Problem.** The pencil skirt parses to 10 steps whose printed numbers are `1, 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 5, 6` — the booklet restarts numbering per diagram group. The app renumbers 1..N, so the giant `STEP 6` on my card is the booklet's "Invisible Zip" under its printed step 4. The moment I glance at the PDF, or at the saved page image, the numbers disagree. The same mismatch appears on the Sundress once two heading-steps are removed.
**Proposal.** Keep `StepRow.n` (already parsed) and render it as a small `pattern step 4` chip in `sw-step-meta` whenever it differs from the app's index; the big number stays the app's position. In the Steps sheet show `6 · (4)`. Detect non-monotonic `n` at parse time and add it to the import warnings.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 3 · **Risks.** None; `n` is already in the data model and thrown away by the UI.

### 12. Suggest my size — and tell me when I'm between two
**Problem.** The Size & alterations sheet has a lovely size-chart table (36–44, Bust/Waist/Hip) and a "My measurements" block that is stored in Settings and reused across projects (exactly right). I typed 97 / 79 / 104 / 168 and **nothing happened** — no highlight, no suggestion, no ease. Choosing a size from a chart and spotting that you're a 42 bust on a 44 hip is the first thing every sewist does with a new pattern, it is pure arithmetic, and no app in the research does it. Also: all four "my measurement" placeholders read "96 cm", including Height, and the values are stored as bare strings (`"97"`), so nothing downstream can tell cm from inches.
**Proposal.** Store `{ value, unit }` for my measurements with a cm/inch segmented control at the top of the block. Once both my measurements and a parsed chart exist, highlight the matching cell per row in the chart, put a banner above the size chips — *"Your measurements suggest **42** · bust 42, waist 42, hip 44 — you may want to blend to 44 at the hip"* — and add a one-tap "Use 42". Add an Ease row on the Finished tab (`finished − body` for the chosen size) when both tables parsed.
**Effort** M · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** Chart cells are strings with mixed units ("84", "103.5", "33 1/4\""); parse leniently and fall back to no suggestion rather than a wrong one. Never auto-select the size.

### 13. Quilt layout: the thing I tap 160 times is not the big button
**Problem.** Loading the quilt into a project gave three unit counters (Blocks 0/20, Flying Geese 0/80, HSTs 0/160) stacked as full-width cards taking **361 px**. Body scroll height 1356 against a 755 px viewport; the STEP DONE button starts at y=915 — completely below the fold. And the hierarchy is inverted: I will spend two evenings inside step 1 making 80 flying geese, tapping a small card, while a 211 px button waits for a step I finish twice a week. Long-press for −1 works but is undiscoverable and its `aria-label` goes stale after a decrement.
**Proposal.** For `kind === 'quilt'`, collapse `sw-units` to a horizontal chip strip (`Geese 0/80 · HSTs 0/160 · Blocks 0/20`) with one **active** unit promoted into the big `.stitch-btn` slot in place of STEP DONE — tap = +1, the step text stays as the card above it, and a small "Step done ✓" link sits where "↺ back a step" is. Tapping another chip swaps the active unit. Add a `−` affordance and a "set to…" on long-press, and fix the aria-label on patch.
**Effort** M · **Impact** 4 · **Risks.** A second layout mode inside `renderProject`; keep it behind `kind === 'quilt' && units.length > 0` and let the user flip back. Also fixes the known ">700 px tall overflow" for the worst case.

### 14. Subcuts are a chain, not six parallel jobs
**Problem.** The quilt parsed `From Fabric A, cut (7) 4 1/2" x WOF strips.` and `Subcut into (56) 4 1/2" squares.` as two independent rows, so the cutting list claims 63 things to cut when it is really 7 strips that then become 56 squares. Worse, `Subcut into (20) 8 7/8" squares, then cut once on the diagonal to yield (40) triangles.` became a row whose *piece name* is `8 7/8" squares, then cut once on the diagonal to yield` — the trailing clause is jammed into the label and the **40 triangles**, the number that actually matters, is lost. And the same document's two ways of saying the same thing give two different names: `4 1/2" x WOF strip` vs `strips 2 1/2" x width of fabric`.
**Proposal.** Give `CutRow` a `parentId`. A `Subcut…` line attaches to the previous row from the same fabric source and renders indented under it ("↳ subcut into 56 × 4½″ squares"), contributing to its own count but not to the top-level piece total. Capture a trailing `to yield (N) <thing>` as a further child row. Normalise the WOF piece name to `<dims> x WOF strip` in both spellings by stripping a leading/trailing `strips?`.
**Effort** M · **Impact** 3 · **Risks.** Needs a real quilt PDF before tuning; the regexes in the spec (families 4 and 5) are close but the "then cut once on the diagonal" tail is unspecified.

### 15. Attach the seam-allowance exception to the steps it applies to
**Problem.** The step card shows `SA 1 cm (3/8") +1`. The `+1` is a sheet with *"Except Neckline, Armhole and pocket edge which are 6mm / 1/4"."* — correctly parsed, well presented, and completely passive. When I am on step 8 (POCKETS) or step 2 (which sews the neckline and armhole), the chip still says 1 cm. That is precisely the case the research called out as the one a naive extractor gets wrong, and the app gets the *parse* right and then throws the value away at the point of use.
**Proposal.** At import, extract the exception's noun phrases (`neckline`, `armhole`, `pocket edge`) and, for each step whose text matches one of them, store `step.saOverrideMm`. Render the chip as `SA 6 mm (1/4") — neckline` in the accent colour on those steps. Show a one-line "why" on tap.
**Effort** M · **Impact** 3 · **Risks.** Over-matching ("press the neckline seam" doesn't need 1/4"). Treat it as a reminder, word it as "this step mentions the neckline — the pattern sews those at 6 mm", not as a fact.

### 16. Machine settings need presets in the box and a route from the step card
**Problem.** The Machine settings sheet is six empty text fields (Needle, Thread, Stitch length, Tension, Presser foot, Notes) and an empty Presets list. Typing "80/12 universal" and "2.5" on a phone with fabric-dusty hands, per project, is exactly the "more time updating the system than sewing" that kills adoption. And the one moment I need it — step 10, invisible zip, "use an invisible zipper foot" — the settings are three taps away behind ⋯.
**Proposal.** Ship five built-in presets (`Quilting cotton`, `Knit / jersey`, `Denim / canvas`, `Silk / fine woven`, `Topstitching`) with sensible needle / stitch length / foot values, offered as chips the first time the sheet is opened; the user's own presets append below. Add a ⚙️ chip next to the SA chip on the step card opening the same sheet, and highlight the preset's foot when the step text names one (`zipper foot`, `walking foot`, `buttonhole`).
**Effort** S–M · **Impact** 3 · **Risks.** Needle/stitch-length defaults are opinions; label them "a starting point — test on a scrap".

### 17. Quilt projects are wearing a garment's chrome
**Problem.** On the quilt project the mini card shows `📏 Pick your size and note alterations` and the ⋯ menu offers Size & alterations — a quilt has no size chart and no FBA. The import warnings said *"No size chart found — add your size by hand"* and *"No notions list found"* for a pattern that correctly has neither. Meanwhile nothing warned that fabric requirements came out empty (see #4), which is the thing that was actually missing. Separately the shell leaks crochet vocabulary into sewing: the Checklist sheet is titled **"Assembly checklist"** with a footer **↻ Reload from "Single piece"**, page-image blobs are keyed `p:<id>:chartpage:N` in one place (cross-stitch's name) and `p:<id>:page:N` in another, and sewing projects still carry `yarnColors`, `countMode` and `groupSize`.
**Proposal.** Make warnings and the mini rows `kind`-aware: for `quilt`, drop the size-chart and notions warnings, add a fabric-requirements one, and replace the size row with `🧵 Finished 60" × 72" · 12" blocks` (parse `Finished Quilt Size` / `Finished Block Size` from the cover — currently not captured at all). Give the craft contract a `checklistTitle` and let the checklist's reload source read "Quilt template". Unify the blob key prefix.
**Effort** S · **Impact** 3 · **Risks.** Purely additive; `kind` is already on `craftData`.

### 18. Tell me how much space the pages are eating, and clean up orphans
**Problem.** The Pages sheet says "stored on this device only — keep the PDF. They are not in your backup file" (good, honest) and offers "Delete the page images". It does not say how big they are. The cross-stitch module already shows `BlobStore.usage()` in its UI; sewing doesn't. And I found a live orphan in this profile — `p:mu61459f-…:chartpage:0` belonging to a project that no longer exists in `localStorage`. `deletePrefix` only runs from `deleteProjectFlow`, so a cleared profile, a restored backup or an interrupted delete leaves image blobs stranded with no way for the user to see or free them.
**Proposal.** Show `usage()` per project in the Pages sheet ("9 pages · 2.1 MB") and a total in Settings → Storage, with a "Free up space" action. On `Store.load()`, sweep `BlobStore.keys('p:')` against the live project ids and delete the strays (debounced, once per session, never blocking first paint).
**Effort** M · **Impact** 2 · **Risks.** Must run after craft registration and after an import, or it will eat the blobs of a project mid-creation. Guard on "projects loaded and non-empty".

## Needs infrastructure

- **A real quilt PDF fixture.** Everything I say about quilting above is reasoned from the research doc plus a synthetic Riley Blake block I wrote and fed to `Sewing.parse`. Items 4, 8, 13, 14 and 17 all need the Riley Blake *Misty Morning* (WOF strips, abbreviation vs spelled-out in one file) and Suzy Quilts *Duval Star* (fat-quarter requirements, 224 flying geese) before anyone tunes a regex. Both are free and ungated per `docs/research/sewing.md` A.4.
- **A bag fixture.** The hardware/interfacing branch (`D-ring`, `swivel hook`, `Pellon SF-101`, `2-1/2 yards`) and the mid-pattern seam-allowance change have only ever seen synthetic text. Robert Kaufman *Forage Bag* and Sew Sweetness *Baker Street Bag* are both direct downloads.
- **`PdfText.extractTables()`** (research B.3.2 option 2). The per-size fabric columns collapse today — the Sundress gives two amounts for five sizes on the 150 cm row and the app back-fills them across the group, which is a sensible guess but a guess. Column clustering on `transform[4]` would also fix multi-size quilt counts like `4 (64, 264)`, which the current parser cannot represent at all.
- **Zip backup including BlobStore.** Already on the handoff list; it is the difference between "my diagrams are on this phone" and "my diagrams survive a new phone", and it is the sewing module's only non-recoverable data.
- **Touch-hardware pass on the page viewer.** Pinch-zoom on a rendered A4 diagram is the whole point of keeping pages, and nothing here has run on a real phone. Same caveat the 3D diagram carries.
- **A craft contract extension** for #9 and #17: optional `emojiSet`, `notesPlaceholder`, `checklistTitle`, and a way for a craft to mount the shared PDF drop zone inside the shell's New project sheet. That is a `docs/CRAFTS.md` change and a small `js/app.js` edit, so it wants to land as its own commit before the sewing-side work.
