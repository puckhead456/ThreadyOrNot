Knitting counter test suite (wave H, knit-counter)

The knitting state on the shared counter, against the real js/store.js: Project.gauge, Part.side / markers / linked / shortRows, their normalisation, templates, backups, undo, and the read helpers the counter widgets use (side, markers, short rows, two at a time, cables, lengths, glossary, charts). Pattern lines are hand-built in the Line shape of docs/wave-h/CONTRACT.md §2 and served by a stand-in parser registered for the craft, so nothing here depends on window.Knits. No PDFs.

This page runs under its own storage key (Store.__setKeyForTests) and removes it when done.
