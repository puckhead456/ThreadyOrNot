Sewing.js fixtures

Runs Sewing.parse against real pattern booklets in ../tmp-pdf/. A fixture is read from sew-<name>.txt when one exists, otherwise the sew-<name>.pdf is extracted here with PdfText and the text is cached in sessionStorage for the rest of the browser session.

Those files are copyrighted, personal-use-only material: tmp-pdf/ is gitignored and nothing from them is asserted verbatim here — only shapes and counts. A fresh clone has no fixtures at all and this page stays green.
