Sewing.js fixtures

Runs Sewing.parse against real pattern text in ../tmp-pdf/sew-*.txt. Those files are copyrighted, personal-use-only material: tmp-pdf/ is gitignored and nothing from them is ever asserted verbatim here — only shapes and counts. A fresh clone has no fixtures at all and this page stays green.

To add one: put the PDF in tmp-pdf/, extract its text with tmp-pdf/extract.html (or the in-app importer), save it as tmp-pdf/sew-<name>.txt, and add an entry to FIXTURES below.
