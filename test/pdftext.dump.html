PdfText dump (dev only - reads ../tmp-pdf at runtime; nothing is committed)

?f=<file.pdf> dumps PdfText.extract per page, then the page's raw pdf.js rows (every item, grouped by baseline only, with its y and height) and the items the extractor did not emit. &route=open dumps open().textOf instead. &pages=3-5 limits the raw section. ?all=1&sink=PORT walks every fixture and POSTs <name>.extract.txt / <name>.raw.txt to a local scratch sink (add &heavy=1 for files over 17 MB, &only=a,b to pick).
